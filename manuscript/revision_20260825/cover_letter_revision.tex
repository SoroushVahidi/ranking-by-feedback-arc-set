Dear Professor Arabnia,

Thank you for the opportunity to revise my manuscript and for the careful reviews for \textit{The Journal of Supercomputing}.
I am submitting the revised manuscript for reconsideration.
The manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not under consideration elsewhere.
I take full responsibility for the scientific content of this revision.

The manuscript has undergone a substantial major revision in response to all four reviewers.
The accompanying point-by-point response addresses every comment.
The principal changes are:

\begin{enumerate}
\item Title and positioning were revised to remove an unqualified scalability claim and to distinguish prior MWFAS components from the contributions of this work.
\item The fixed-topological reinsertion proxy was replaced in the canonical pipeline by exact reachability-aware reinsertion.
\item Principal Tables~4--6 now evaluate the canonical reachability-aware method, \textsc{OURS-Reach}.
\item Inconsistent baseline tables identified by Reviewer~3 were rebuilt from a single canonical data source.
\item Direct classical runtime comparisons were added; the method is slower than most lightweight classical estimators while retaining a substantial end-to-end advantage over the evaluated trained GNN methods.
\item Structural ablations, sensitivity, timeout/coverage, family-aware, and statistical analyses were added.
\item Theory was strengthened (ranking--MWFAS equivalence, reachability correctness, Demetrescu--Finocchi guarantee boundaries, fallback impossibility, and a secondary weighted min-cut repair).
\item The manuscript was substantially rewritten to reduce repetition and improve readability.
\end{enumerate}

I am grateful to you and the reviewers; their comments led to substantial improvements.
Detailed responses appear in the accompanying response-to-reviewers document.
