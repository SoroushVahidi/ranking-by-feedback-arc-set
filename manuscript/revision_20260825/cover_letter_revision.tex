Dear Editor,

Thank you for the opportunity to revise my manuscript, now entitled
\textit{``Training-Free Ranking from Pairwise Comparisons via Acyclic Graph Construction,''}
and for the careful reviews for \textit{The Journal of Supercomputing}.
I am submitting the revised manuscript for reconsideration.
The manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not under consideration elsewhere.
I take full responsibility for the scientific content of this revision.

The manuscript has undergone a substantial major revision in response to all four reviewers.
The accompanying point-by-point response addresses every comment.
The principal changes are:

\begin{enumerate}
\item The title and positioning were revised to remove an unqualified scalability claim and to distinguish prior MWFAS components from the contributions of this work.

\item The submitted fixed-topological reinsertion proxy was replaced in the canonical pipeline by exact reachability-aware reinsertion.

\item Principal Tables~4--6 now evaluate the canonical reachability-aware method, \textsc{OURS-Reach}, rather than the submitted topology-proxy variant.

\item The inconsistent baseline tables identified by Reviewer~3 were rebuilt from a single canonical data source.

\item Direct runtime comparisons against training-free classical baselines were added.
The manuscript states that the method is slower than most lightweight classical methods, while retaining a substantial end-to-end runtime advantage over the evaluated trained GNN methods.

\item Structural ablations, sensitivity analyses, timeout/coverage analysis, family-aware analysis, and statistical tests were added.

\item Theory was strengthened with ranking--MWFAS optimum-value equivalence, exact reachability correctness, Demetrescu--Finocchi guarantee-boundary clarification, a fallback impossibility result, and a secondary weighted min-cut exchange with guarantees.

\item The manuscript was substantially rewritten to reduce repetition and improve readability.
\end{enumerate}

I am grateful to the editor and reviewers; their comments led to substantial improvements.
Detailed responses appear in the accompanying response-to-reviewers document.
